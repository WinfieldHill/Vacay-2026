      Where everything is

      Two destinations, both a nonstop flight away

      The four Bahamas places sit on one small island, and the four Mexico resorts sit within a few miles of each other on the Riviera Maya. The three Atlantis hotels and Comfort Suites are all within about a 15-minute walk of one another on Paradise Island.

      Before you book

      The practical list

      Passports

      Both girls need passport books. Both are under 16, so both parents sign and the application is in person. Child passports last 5 years, so check the dates on any they already hold.

      Timing

      Buy flights by November or December. There is one American nonstop a day to Nassau, and its cheap seats go first. Book refundable hotel rates now and re-check them.

      Seaweed

      2026 was a heavy sargassum year on Mexico's Caribbean coast. All four Mexico resorts sit near Playa del Carmen, where it tends to be heavier. Nassau is essentially clear.

      Water and weather

      Riviera Maya: mid-80s, sea about 78–80°F. Nassau: around 80, sea about 75–77°F.

      Fees to expect

      Mexico: visitor tax of about $16 a person. Bahamas: 20–21% room tax, $76–83 nightly resort fees, and 10% VAT plus 15% gratuity on restaurant checks.

      Not in any total

      Parking at PHL, travel insurance and the cat sitter.
